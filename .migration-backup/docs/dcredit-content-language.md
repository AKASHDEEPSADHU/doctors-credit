# DCredit content language (internal)

Use this guide for customer-facing copy. It is not a public page.

## Positioning

Doctor's Credit is an international planned-care decision and coordination platform. It helps people evaluate whether India may be worth investigating for care they are already considering.

Central question: could India be the right place for the care you are considering?

The decision is quality-first. Cost is one factor among several. A lower quoted treatment price does not automatically make a medical journey better.

## Preferred terms

- international patients
- planned care
- cross-border care
- home country
- healthcare coverage or funding
- private, public or self-funded care
- what you may need to pay yourself
- total journey
- clinical expertise
- hospital capability
- quality and safety
- continuity of care
- worth investigating
- may make sense
- provider-level evidence
- selected tertiary centres

## Avoid as default language

- medical tourism (unless discussing industry or government terminology)
- Medical Value Travel as a substitute for patient-facing language
- cheap treatment / cheap surgery
- world-class (as a blanket claim)
- best / world's best
- guaranteed savings or guaranteed outcomes
- US patients / US-focused (unless the sentence is actually about the United States)
- See how much you can save

## Recurring editorial ideas (use sparingly)

- India should be considered on more than price.
- Cost can open the conversation. It should not end it.
- A treatment price is only one part of a medical journey.
- Quality deserves to be investigated directly.
- The journey does not end when treatment ends.
- Capabilities vary by hospital, department and physician.
- Accreditation is useful information, but it is not a guarantee of outcome.

## Country examples

If a fact is about the United States, say so. Do not present U.S. plan math, deductibles or 911 as if they apply everywhere.

When a broad audience needs naming once: people considering care from the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia, Africa, the Middle East or elsewhere. Do not repeat that list throughout the site.

## Claims we do not make

India has the best doctors. India has the world's best hospitals. India is better than the United States, Canada or Europe. India is always cheaper. Lower cost means lower risk. Guaranteed outcomes.

## Claims we can make carefully

India has major tertiary-care hospitals and specialized centres. Some Indian institutions have developed extensive expertise in complex procedures. Selected hospitals offer advanced technologies such as robotic surgery and proton therapy. Cost is one factor among several. Capabilities vary significantly.

## Punctuation

Do not use the em dash character. Use commas, periods, colons or parentheses.

## Preserved product truths

DCredit is not a hospital. DCredit does not diagnose. DCredit does not provide emergency care. Savings are not guaranteed. India is not automatically the right answer. Provider information should be verified before publication. DCredit should not pressure people to travel.
